**Fleet Activity Logs** list what's happened across your fleet, newest first. Supervisors and above can find them under **Fleet > Fleet Activity Logs**.

  **Screenshot needed:** The Activity Logs page with several kinds of entries.
</Note>

## What's logged

| Entry | When it's written |
| --- | --- |
| **Assigned** / **Unassigned** | A driver is put on or taken off a bus, including temporary assignments. |
| **Spawned** / **Despawned** | A bus is spawned or despawned in game. |
| **Refueled** | A bus is refueled in game, with the amount. |
| **Mechanical issue reported** | A player reports a problem with a bus. This also opens a [maintenance record](/fleet/maintenance-logs). |
| **Mechanical issue detected** | The game detects a fault on a bus. This also opens a maintenance record. |
| **Route active** / **Route inactive** | A bus starts or stops running a route. |

Bus locations are recorded too, for the [Live Map View](/maps/live-map) and [Fleet Replay](/fleet/replay), but they aren't listed here.

Most entries come from your game, so your game needs to be [connected to FleetPoint](/connect).

## Activity sessions

An activity session is one stretch of a bus being used in a server. Click **View Session** on any entry to see only that session's entries, and **Show Whole Fleet** to go back.

Each entry also has **View Bus** and **View Profile** buttons.

## How long logs are kept

Logs are kept for 7 days, or 6 months with Extended History. See [Plans](/plans#history).
