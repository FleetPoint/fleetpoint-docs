**Manage Fleet** lists every bus in your company. Everyone can open it from **Fleet > Manage Fleet**. Admins can add and import buses.

<Note>
  **Screenshot needed:** The Manage Fleet table with a few buses, showing each status.
</Note>

## The fleet table

The table shows each bus's status, number, assigned driver, year, make, model, fuel type, and map and lot. Click a column header to sort, search by bus number, and click a row to open the [bus's page](/fleet/buses).

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **In Service** | The bus can be driven. |
| **Spare** | The bus is in service and kept as a spare. Spares are picked first when a driver needs a replacement bus. |
| **Out of Service** | The bus can't be driven or spawned. |

On the Free plan, only the buses your plan covers are shown. See [Plans](/plans).

## Adding a bus

Admins can click **Add Bus** to add a single bus.

- **Vehicle**: Bus Number, Year, Make, Model, Fuel Type, and whether it's **In Service** and a **Spare**.
- **Placement**: the Map, its Spawner Category, and optionally a Lot.
- **Service Record** (Prime): Engine, Transmission, Mileage, and Engine Hours.
- **Details**: the Assigned Driver's Roblox ID, the Bus Image ID, and the Model Asset ID the spawner spawns.
- **Note** (Prime): without Prime, the note is set for you from the bus's year, make, and model.

A map's spawner needs at least one [spawner category](/maps/manage-maps#spawner-categories) before buses can be added to it.

## Importing buses

If you used the GTS Assets Spawner, you can bring your whole fleet over at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import the spawner configuration first">
    Your map's spawner categories need to match your fleet's. [Import your spawner configuration](/maps/manage-maps#importing-a-spawner-configuration) onto the map before importing buses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export your fleet">
    In Roblox Studio, use the [Spawner Data Exporter](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/122199457306830/Spawner-Data-Exporter) plugin and choose **Export Spawner Fleet Data**. Copy everything it exports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into FleetPoint">
    Click **Import Buses** and paste it into **Spawner Configuration**. FleetPoint checks it and shows how its categories match up with your map's.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where the buses go">
    Choose the map, and optionally a lot (Prime) to park every imported bus in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Click **Import**, then check your fleet afterwards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

How the import reads your configuration:

- Year, make, and model are read from each bus's model name, e.g. "2022 Blue Bird Vision". Buses without a year are given 1900, and you'll be warned so you can fix them.
- Fuel type is guessed from the bus number. Check it after importing.
- Whether each bus is in service, a spare, its assigned driver, image, model asset ID, cosmetics, and description (Prime) are copied over.
- Buses whose numbers are already on the map are skipped, not updated.
- Up to 500 buses can be imported at once.

<Warning>
  The import can get some things wrong. Always double-check your fleet after importing.
</Warning>

</Note>

## Changing several buses at once

Select buses with the checkboxes, then open the **⋯** menu in the table header.

| Action | Minimum role |
| --- | --- |
| Put in service / Take out of service | Supervisor |
| Unassign drivers | Supervisor |
| Update year, make, model, or fuel type | Admin |
| Update lot (buses on one map, Prime) | Admin |
| Delete buses | Admin |

Up to 500 buses can be changed at once. To delete buses, you'll need to type the confirmation shown, e.g. "I wish to delete 3 buses". Deleting buses also deletes their logs, maintenance records, and activity sessions.

## Changing one bus

Open the **⋯** menu on a row to update a bus's year, make, model, fuel type, or lot (Admin), take it in or out of service (Mechanic), unassign its driver (Supervisor), or copy its bus ID (everyone).

For everything else, open the [bus's page](/fleet/buses).
