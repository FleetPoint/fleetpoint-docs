**Fleet Replay** plays back a bus's past activity sessions on your map. Supervisors and above can find it under **Fleet > Fleet Replay**.

<Info>
  Requires [Prime](/plans). Your map needs a [map image and bounds](/maps/manage-maps#map-image) before a replay can be drawn on it.
</Info>

## Choosing a session

The Fleet Replay page lists finished activity sessions, newest first. Each one shows the bus, the map, who was driving, when it started and ended, how long it lasted, and its mileage. Click **View Replay** to open it.

If a session shows **(last seen)** next to its end time, the game didn't close it properly. The time shown is when the bus was last heard from.

<Note>
  **Screenshot needed:** The Fleet Replay list of sessions.
</Note>

## Watching a replay

The map shows the bus's whole route as a faint line, with the part driven so far in bold, along with your map's points of interest and lots.

- Use the play button and seekbar to move through the session. Marks on the seekbar show events like spawns, refuels, and reported issues.
- Change the speed with **1×**, **2×**, **4×**, **8×**, or **16×**.
- Hover over a point on the route to see the time and speed there.

The side panel shows the bus, the driver, average and maximum speed, mileage driven, duration, and how many entries were logged.

<Note>
  **Screenshot needed:** A replay mid-playback, showing the route, seekbar, and side panel.
</Note>

## How long replays are kept

Sessions, and so their replays, are kept for 7 days, or 6 months with Extended History. See [Plans](/plans#history).
