title: "Set Up Inspections"
description: "Get pre-trip, post-trip, and mechanic inspections running for your company."

---

This guide walks through everything an admin sets up before drivers and mechanics start inspecting buses.

<Info>
  Inspections require [Prime](/plans).
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write your checklists">
    Open [Inspections](/fleet/inspections) and click **Edit Checklists**. Every company starts with a default checklist for each type. Change them to match how your company inspects buses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide which items are scanned">
    Make an item scanned when it's a physical part of the bus with several things to check, like the instrument cluster or a corner of the exterior. Put its components on the lines below it, each starting with a dash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag your bus models">
    For every scanned item, add a tag to your bus models with exactly the same name as the item.

    <Info>
      **Coming soon:** Details on adding inspection tags to your bus models will be added once the in-game tablet is released.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose inspection types per map">
    In each map's [settings](/maps/manage-maps#driver-inspections), choose whether drivers do **Pre-trip** and **Post-trip** inspections there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide if inspections can be done on the website">
    On the Inspections page, keep **Website inspections** ticked to let mechanics file inspections on the website by signing off, or untick it so inspections can only be done in game.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track when mechanic inspections are due">
    In [Fleet Health](/fleet/fleet-health#settings) **Settings**, tick **Track upcoming mechanic inspections** and set how many days apart they should be. Buses that are due or overdue show up under **Upcoming Inspections**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
