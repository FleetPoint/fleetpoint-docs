---
title: "Lay Out Your Map"
description: "Add your map image, points of interest, lots, and routes so you can see your fleet on the map."
icon: "map-location-dot"
---

This guide sets up everything that appears on your map in FleetPoint, so the [Live Map View](/maps/live-map) and [Fleet Replay](/fleet/replay) have something to draw on.

<Info>
  Map images, points of interest, lots, and routes all require [Prime](/plans).
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your map image and bounds">
    Upload a top-down image of your map to Roblox and add it to your [map settings](/maps/manage-maps#map-image), along with the in-game coordinates of its corners. Use the preview to line it up.

    <Note>
      **Image needed:** An example of a top-down map image, with its top-left and bottom-right corners marked and the in-game coordinates for each.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark points of interest">
    On the same page, drop pins on places like depots, schools, and terminals, and name them. See [Points of interest](/maps/manage-maps#points-of-interest).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draw your lots">
    Create a [lot](/maps/lots) for each place buses park, and draw its bounds on the map.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Park your buses">
    Put your buses in their lots from [Manage Fleet](/fleet/manage-fleet#changing-several-buses-at-once) with **Update lot**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your routes">
    Add your [routes](/maps/routes), using your points of interest and lots as stops, and choose who else can add routes on the map.
  </Step>
</Steps>
