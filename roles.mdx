Everyone in your company has one of four roles. Each role can do everything the roles below it can.

| Role | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Driver** | Opens the dashboard. Views the fleet, buses, active buses, and routes. |
| **Mechanic** | Takes buses in and out of service, and works on maintenance, inspections, and fleet health. |
| **Supervisor** | Assigns drivers, and views the live map, activity logs, and replays. |
| **Admin** | Everything, including the fleet, maps, lots, settings, and subscriptions. |

## How roles are assigned

Roles come from your members' ranks in your Roblox group. Admins choose the minimum rank for each role on the company [Overview](/company/overview#roles) page:

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum Driver Rank | 1 |
| Minimum Mechanic Rank | 150 |
| Minimum Supervisor Rank | 200 |
| Minimum Admin Rank | 250 |

A member gets the highest role their rank reaches. Members ranked below the Minimum Driver Rank can't open your company at all.

FleetPoint checks ranks every time a page loads, so promoting or demoting someone in your Roblox group changes their role in FleetPoint right away.

| Page | Minimum role | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Overview](/company/overview) | Driver (admins see settings) | All |
| [Subscriptions](/company/subscriptions) | Admin | All |
| [Partner Program](/company/partner-program) | Admin | All |
| [Active Drivers & Buses](/company/active) | Driver | All |
| [Manage Fleet](/fleet/manage-fleet) | Driver | All |
| [Fleet Activity Logs](/fleet/activity-logs) | Supervisor | All |

| [Fleet Health](/fleet/fleet-health) | Mechanic | All |
| [Inspections](/fleet/inspections) | Mechanic | Prime |

Pages your role can't open are hidden from the sidebar.

## Common actions

| Action | Minimum role |
| --- | --- |
| Take one bus in or out of service | Mechanic |
| Take several buses in or out of service at once | Supervisor |
| Assign, unassign, or temporarily reassign drivers | Supervisor |
| Log, edit, and inspect maintenance records | Mechanic |
| Delete maintenance records | Admin |
| File inspections | Mechanic |
| Edit inspection checklists and settings | Admin |
| Add, import, edit, or delete buses | Admin |
| Create and change maps, lots, and spawner settings | Admin |
| Create routes | Depends on the map's [route creation policy](/maps/routes) |
| Approve or reject routes | Supervisor |
