---
title: "Active Drivers & Buses"
description: "See which buses are being driven right now."
icon: "signal-stream"
---

**Active Drivers & Buses** lists every bus currently in use in your game, newest first. Everyone in your company can open it from **Company > Active Drivers & Buses**.

Each bus shows:

- Its bus number and map.
- Its driver, if anyone is driving it.
- The Roblox server it's in.

With [Prime](/plans), click **View Live Map** to see the bus on the [Live Map View](/maps/live-map). The Live Map View needs the Supervisor role or higher.
