The **Subscriptions** page shows your company's **FleetPoint Prime** and **FleetPoint Extended History** subscriptions. Admins can find it under **Company > Subscriptions**.

<Note>
  **Screenshot needed:** The Subscriptions page with an active Prime subscription.
</Note>

## Managing your subscriptions

Subscriptions are bought, renewed, and cancelled on Roblox. Click **Manage your Subscriptions on Roblox** to open the FleetPoint experience and manage them there.

## Subscription status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **ACTIVE** | The subscription is active and will renew. |
| **EXPIRING SOON** | The subscription is active but won't renew. |
| **EXPIRED** | The subscription has ended. |
| **INACTIVE** | Your company doesn't have this subscription. |

For each subscription, you'll also see its subscription ID, who subscribed, when it was activated, when it was last billed, when it next bills, and when it expires.

See [Plans](/plans) for what each subscription includes, and for what happens when one ends.
