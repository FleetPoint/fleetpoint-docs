The Partner Program recognizes established communities that run their fleets on FleetPoint. Admins can find it under **Company > Partner Program**.

<Note>
  **Screenshot needed:** The Partner Program page showing benefits and the requirements checklist.
</Note>

## Benefits

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Discounted Prime" icon="badge-percent">
    A reduced rate on your Prime subscription.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Custom vanity link" icon="link">
    Open your company's dashboard with a link using your company's name instead of its group ID.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Public company page" icon="globe">
    A page on the FleetPoint website showing off your company to drivers and the community.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Partner badge" icon="award">
    Your company shows as a FleetPoint partner across the dashboard.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Requirements

- **5,000+ Roblox group members.** The Partner Program page checks this for you.
- **500+ Discord server members.**
- **Active for at least 3 months.** Your group has been actively running for the past 3 months or longer.
- **Follows the Roblox Terms of Service.** Your group, games, and Discord server follow Roblox's Terms of Service and Community Standards.
- **Not impersonating another company.** Your name, branding, and group aren't made to look like another company's, including very similar names.

<Warning>
  Every company is considered case by case. Meeting the requirements doesn't guarantee approval, and we can approve or deny any application for any reason.
</Warning>

## Applying

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the requirements">
    Open **Partner Program** in your dashboard. It shows whether your group meets the member requirement. The other requirements are reviewed when you apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email us">
    Click **Email support@fleetpointapp.com**. This opens an email with your group's name, link, and member count already filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your details">
    Fill in your Discord invite, Discord member count, game link, and a short description of your company, then send it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also email [support@fleetpointapp.com](mailto:support@fleetpointapp.com) directly.

Once you're a partner, the Partner Program page shows that your company is a FleetPoint partner.
