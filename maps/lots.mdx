Lots are areas on a map where buses are parked, like a depot or a yard. Admins can find them under **Maps > Manage Lots**.

<Info>
  Requires [Prime](/plans).
</Info>

<Note>
  **Screenshot needed:** The Manage Lots page with lots on a map.
</Note>

## Creating a lot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new lot">
    Click **Create Lot** under the map it belongs on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter the lot's name and choose its map. Lots can't be moved between maps later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draw its bounds (optional)">
    If the map has a [map image and bounds](/maps/manage-maps#map-image), draw a rectangle around the lot with the tools in the bottom-left corner of the map.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A lot needs bounds to show on the live map and replays, and to be used as a [route](/maps/routes) stop.

<Note>
  **Screenshot needed:** Drawing a lot's bounds on the map.
</Note>

## Parking buses in a lot

Buses are put in lots from the fleet, not from the lot's page:

- One bus: **Assign Lot** on the [bus's page](/fleet/buses#lots).
- Several buses: **Update lot** in [Manage Fleet](/fleet/manage-fleet#changing-several-buses-at-once).
- When adding or importing buses.

Each lot's card shows how many buses are parked in it.

## Editing and deleting a lot

Click **Manage Lot** to rename it or redraw its bounds. Deleting a lot takes its buses out of it, but they stay on the map.
