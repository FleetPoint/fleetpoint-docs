Routes are the paths your buses run, made up of stops in order. Find them under **Maps > Manage Routes**.

<Info>
  Requires [Prime](/plans). Everyone can view routes. Who can add them depends on each map's policy.
</Info>

<Note>
  **Screenshot needed:** The Manage Routes page with routes on a map, including one awaiting approval.
</Note>

## Who can add routes

Admins choose who can add routes on each map with **Who can add routes**:

| Policy | Who can add routes |
| --- | --- |
| **Supervisors and administrators only** (default) | Supervisors and admins. |
| **Any driver** | Everyone. Routes go live straight away. |
| **Any driver, with supervisor approval** | Everyone. A supervisor has to approve drivers' routes before they go live. |

- Supervisors and admins can always add, change, and delete any route, and their routes go live straight away.
- Drivers and mechanics can only change routes they made themselves. With approval required, they can't change a route once it's live, and any change sends it back for approval.

## Creating a route

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a route">
    Click **Add Route** under the map.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and number it">
    Enter a **Name** and a **Route Number** from 1 to 99,999. The route number is the tag a bus displays, and must be unique on the map. The next free number is filled in for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add stops">
    Add at least 2 stops, up to 250, in the order buses should follow them:

    - **Free point**: drop a pin anywhere with the pin tool.
    - **Point of interest**: click one of the map's [points of interest](/maps/manage-maps#points-of-interest) and choose **Add as stop**.
    - **Lot**: stop at a [lot](/maps/lots)'s center. Only lots with bounds can be used.

    Reorder stops with the arrows, or remove them with the trash icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add Route**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Stops at a point of interest or lot move with it. Dragging one of these stops turns it into a free point.

<Warning>
  If a point of interest is renamed or removed, or a lot is deleted or loses its bounds, the stops that used it are dropped the next time the route is saved.
</Warning>

<Info>
  Free points need the map to have a [map image and bounds](/maps/manage-maps#map-image). Without them, stops can only be added from points of interest and lots.
</Info>

<Note>
  **Screenshot needed:** The route editor with several stops on the map.
</Note>

## Approving routes

When drivers' routes need approval, supervisors and admins see **Awaiting approval** on them, with two options:

- **Approve** puts the route live.
- **Request changes** sends it back to whoever made it, with an optional note. It shows **Changes requested** until they update it and send it back.

## Deleting a route

Click the trash icon on a route to delete it. It's deleted immediately.
