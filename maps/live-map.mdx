The **Live Map View** shows where every active bus is right now. Supervisors and above can find it under **Maps > Live Map View**.

<Info>
  Requires [Prime](/plans). The map needs a [map image and bounds](/maps/manage-maps#map-image), and your game needs to be [connected to FleetPoint](/connect).
</Info>

Choose a map from the dropdown. You'll see:

- Each active bus, with an arrow showing which way it's heading. Click a bus to see its number and speed.
- Your map's points of interest and lots. Click one to see its name.

The map updates every 15 seconds.

You can also open the live map from a bus on [Active Drivers & Buses](/company/active) with **View Live Map**.
