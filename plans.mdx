FleetPoint has two plans: **Free** and **Prime**. You can also add **Extended History** to keep your history for longer.

## Free and Prime

| | Free | Prime |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | 0 Robux/month | 1,999 Robux/month |
| Maps | 1 | Unlimited |
| Buses | Up to 150 | Unlimited |
| Lots | — | ✓ |
| Live Map View | — | ✓ |
| Fleet Replay | — | ✓ |
| Routes | — | ✓ |
| Inspections | — | ✓ |
| Fleet Health score | ✓ | ✓ |
| Buses Needing Maintenance and Upcoming Inspections | — | ✓ |
| Maintenance Logs | ✓ | ✓ |
| Activity Logs | ✓ | ✓ |
| Service records and bus notes | — | ✓ |
| Automatic driver reassignment | — | ✓ |

Features that need Prime show a **Prime** tag in the dashboard sidebar, or appear blurred with a link to upgrade.

## What the Free plan covers

On the Free plan, FleetPoint covers:

- **Your first map.** This is the oldest map your company created. Other maps are hidden until you subscribe to Prime.
- **The first 150 buses on that map.** These are the oldest buses by the date they were added. Buses past the first 150 are hidden.

You can't add or import buses that would take your fleet past 150 on the Free plan.

## Subscribing

Subscriptions are bought and managed on Roblox. An admin can open **Company > Subscriptions** and click **Manage your Subscriptions on Roblox**. See [Subscriptions](/company/subscriptions).

## History

FleetPoint keeps your fleet's history for **7 days**. With **Extended History**, it's kept for **6 months**.

This covers:

- Vehicle logs (see [Activity Logs](/fleet/activity-logs)).
- Activity sessions and their [replays](/fleet/replay).
- **Resolved** maintenance records. Open records are always kept.

Anything older is deleted automatically.

## When a subscription ends

When Prime or Extended History expires, you have a **14-day grace period** to renew.

<Warning>
  **This deletion is permanent and CANNOT be undone.** If Prime isn't renewed within the 14-day grace period, your company is moved to the Free plan and everything the Free plan doesn't cover is permanently deleted:

  - Every map except your first one, with its routes and spawner configuration.
  - All lots.
  - Buses past the first 150 on your first map, with their logs, maintenance records, and activity sessions.

  Renewing Prime afterwards does **not** bring any of it back. Drivers on temporary assignments involving deleted buses are moved back to their own buses first.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  If Extended History isn't renewed within the 14-day grace period, all history older than 7 days is **permanently deleted**. This **CANNOT be undone**, even if you renew.
</Warning>
